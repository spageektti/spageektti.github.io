I'm Wiktor Perskawiec, a student from Poland. On the internet, I've been using the name **spageektti**.

## Computer science and mathematics

Much of my time revolves around computer science, whether that's solving algorithmic problems, developing software or experimenting with hardware.

I'm a medalist of the Junior Olympiad in Informatics. Competitive programming has been an opportunity to explore computer science beyond the school curriculum and meet people with similar interests.

A more detailed record of my competition results,
distinctions and related press coverage can be found
on my [achievements page](/about/achievements/).

I'm also a maintainer of [tldr-pages](https://github.com/tldr-pages/tldr), an open-source project providing practical, community-maintained documentation for command-line tools.

## Outside technology

I enjoy reading, travelling and solving puzzles. I'm interested in exploring subjects that have little to do with my usual technical interests.

Not everything I do needs to become a project, although sometimes it ends up becoming one anyway.

## This website

This is my own place on the internet, independent of any particular social media platform.

I intend to use it to document projects, share experiences, write about things that interest me and occasionally look back at what I've been doing.
