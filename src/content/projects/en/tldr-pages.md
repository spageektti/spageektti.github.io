The tldr-pages project aims to make command-line documentation more accessible through concise, practical examples.

## My involvement

I'm a maintainer and member of the project. I contribute to its documentation, tooling and ongoing development.

Working on tldr-pages has given me experience collaborating with an international open-source community and maintaining software used by other people.

## About the project

Rather than attempting to replace traditional manual pages, tldr-pages complements them with examples of common use cases.
